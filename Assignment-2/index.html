A small project with a wide horizon

Stories begin when we pay attention.

Field Notes is a quiet corner for collecting the places, details, and ideas that make an ordinary day worth remembering.

01 Our beginning

Why I created this site

I created Field Notes to make room for the small observations that usually disappear into the rush of a day. A handwritten note, a memorable street, or a new idea can become the beginning of something meaningful when it has a place to live.

This project is also an opportunity to practice building a web experience that feels personal and welcoming. I wanted the page to be simple to navigate, easy to read, and expressive without becoming distracting.

02 The motivation

Curiosity is a good reason to begin.

I felt motivated to create this site because the best learning happens when a project connects technical practice with something human. Designing the structure, choosing a color palette, and shaping the writing all helped me think about how a visitor should feel while exploring a page.

Field Notes is intentionally unfinished in the best way: it can grow with new stories, photographs, and experiments. For now, it is a starting point and a reminder that thoughtful work often begins with noticing one small thing.
